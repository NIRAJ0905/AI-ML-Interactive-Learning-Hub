01 / Foundations

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.

🧠

Reasoning

Using available information and logical rules to reach conclusions or solve problems.

👁️

Perception

Understanding visual, audio and other sensory information from the environment.

💬

Language Understanding

Processing and generating human language through Natural Language Processing (NLP).

🎯

Decision Making

Evaluating possible actions and selecting an appropriate response to a situation.

02 / Foundations

What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables computers to identify patterns in data and improve their performance on a task without being explicitly programmed with every individual rule.

📈

Learning from Data

ML algorithms use examples to identify relationships and patterns.

⚙️

Making Predictions

Trained models use learned patterns to make predictions or decisions on new data.

Produces a prediction or classification.

How does training happen?

The network calculates a prediction, compares it with the expected output using a loss function, and adjusts its weights using backpropagation and an optimization algorithm such as gradient descent. This process repeats over training data.

07 / Critical Thinking

Benefits & Limitations of AI

AI offers powerful capabilities, but its effectiveness depends on the quality of data, design, implementation and human oversight.

✅

Benefits

  • Automates repetitive tasks.
  • Processes large datasets quickly.
  • Can improve consistency in specific tasks.
  • Supports faster decision-making.
  • Enables personalized services.
  • Assists scientific research and innovation.
⚠️

Limitations

  • Can inherit bias from training data.
  • May produce inaccurate outputs.
  • Requires suitable data and computing resources.
  • Can create privacy and security risks.
  • May lack human context and judgment.
  • Can affect employment and existing workflows.

Important Takeaway

AI should be treated as a tool that supports human capabilities, not as an unquestionable replacement for human judgment.

08 / Responsible Technology

Responsible AI, Ethics & Privacy

Responsible AI means designing, developing and deploying AI systems in ways that respect people, reduce harm and promote accountability.

⚖️

Fairness

Identify and reduce unfair bias that may affect individuals or groups.

🔐

Privacy

Protect personal information and use data responsibly with appropriate safeguards.

🔍

Transparency

Make AI capabilities, limitations and decision processes understandable where possible.

👤

Accountability

Ensure people and organizations remain responsible for AI system outcomes.

🛡️

Safety

Test systems for harmful behavior, reliability and security vulnerabilities.

🤝

Human Oversight

Keep appropriate human review and intervention in important decisions.

Example: Bias in AI recruitment

If a recruitment model is trained on historical hiring data containing discriminatory patterns, it may reproduce those patterns. Auditing data and model outcomes helps reduce this risk.
